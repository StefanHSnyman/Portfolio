About

I'm an Information Technology student at North-West University (Vanderbijlpark campus) with a clear direction: networking, infrastructure and network security. Where most of my classmates met networking in a textbook, I met it in a rack — my homelab runs segmented VLANs behind pfSense, virtualised services on Proxmox, and PRTG watching all of it.

My approach to problems is an engineer's: design the topology first, segment the failure domains, then build — and monitor everything, because a network you can't observe is a network you can't trust. The same thinking carries into my software work in C#, Python and SQL, where I've built full database-backed applications.

A week shadowing a Core Network Engineer at Reunert Connect confirmed the direction. Next milestones: graduate in 2027, then the MTCNA certification — and a junior role in network engineering or infrastructure where I can keep building at production scale.
